# Open data · Calendario del Fuego

The tables behind the app, as CSV (UTF-8, comma separator, decimal point). The two large files come as a CSV inside
a `.zip`: open them with a double click, or directly with `pandas.read_csv("file.zip")`.
Generated by `pipeline/paso7_datos_abiertos.py`. *Versión en español: [README.md](README.md).*

**Unit.** `hotspots` are **MODIS-equivalent hot spots**: cell-days (a 1 km square with at least one hot spot on a
day) converted to what the Aqua satellite would have seen. They are not fires or hectares. Months converted from VIIRS
are estimates and may have decimals.

**Normal.** The median of that month over 2003–2022, the years observed by Aqua.

## Files

### `serie_mensual_pais_provincias.csv`
One row per area, year and month. Country total and 24 provinces, since 2003.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `level` | `country` or `province` |
| `area_code` | INDEC/IGN province code (2 digits); `00` is the country |
| `area` | Name |
| `year`, `month` | Year and month |
| `hotspots` | MODIS-equivalent hot spots in the month |
| `lo90`, `hi90` | 90 % range (months converted from VIIRS only; empty for Aqua-observed months) |
| `median_month` | Normal for that month (2003–2022 median) |
| `p90_month` | 90th percentile of that month over 2003–2022: above it, the month is unusual |
| `rank` | Rank of the month among all years (1 = highest) |
| `n_years` | Number of years compared in `rank` |
| `prob_unusual` | Probability that the month exceeds the 90th percentile, given the range (converted months) |
| `unusual` | 1 if the month is classified as unusual |
| `complete_month` | 0 if the month is in progress (do not compare with normal) |
| `source` | `aqua_observed` (2003–2022), `snpp_converted` (2023–2025) or `noaa20_converted` (2026) |

### `serie_mensual_departamentos.zip`
Same as above, by department. No range or rank: departments use their province's factor.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `province_code`, `province` | Province |
| `department_code`, `department` | IGN department code (5 digits) and name |
| `year`, `month`, `hotspots`, `median_month`, `complete_month`, `source` | As above |

### `serie_diaria.zip`
Harmonized daily series. **Only days with hot spots are listed**: a missing day is zero.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `level` | `country`, `province` or `department` |
| `area_code` | `00`, province code or department code |
| `date` | Date (YYYY-MM-DD). The last day may be incomplete |
| `hotspots` | MODIS-equivalent hot spots on the day |

### `factores_conversion.csv`
The step 4 factors: how many Aqua cell-days correspond to one Suomi NPP cell-day, by province, season and day or
night. Learned on 2012–2022.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `province_code`, `province`, `region` | Province and the region it leans on when it has little data |
| `season` | Quarter: `DEF` (Dec–Feb), `MAM`, `JJA`, `SON` |
| `day_night` | `day` or `night` |
| `aqua_cell_days`, `snpp_cell_days` | Cell-days of each satellite in the learning period |
| `factor` | Factor used: MODIS equivalent = VIIRS cell-days × `factor` |
| `viirs_per_modis` | Its inverse: how many times more VIIRS sees |
| `weight_own_data` | Weight (0 to 1) of the province's own data against its region |
| `viirs_per_modis_province_only`, `viirs_per_modis_region` | The ratio from the province's data alone, and the region's |
| `noaa20_to_snpp` | Ratio that takes NOAA-20 to Suomi NPP before applying the factor |
| `model` | Model chosen in validation |

### `clima_mensual.csv`
Climate by province and country total (NASA POWER, MERRA-2 reanalysis; mean of the grid points in the area).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `area_code`, `area`, `year`, `month` | Area and month |
| `rain_mm` | Rain in the month (mm) |
| `humidity_pct` | Mean relative humidity at 2 m (%) |
| `tmax_c` | Maximum temperature of the month: the month's highest (°C), averaged over the area's grid points |
| `rain_prev3_mm`, `rain_prev6_mm` | Rain accumulated over the 3 and 6 **previous** months |
| `complete_month` | 0 if the month is in progress |

### `anual_pais_comparacion.csv`
The country's yearly total three ways, to see why harmonization is needed.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `year` | Year (complete years) |
| `harmonized` | Harmonized series |
| `aqua_only` | Aqua alone, unconverted (from 2023 it passes later and stops being comparable) |
| `all_sensors_summed` | All hot spots from all satellites, summed as they come |
